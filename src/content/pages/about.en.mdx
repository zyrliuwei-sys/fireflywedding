## An independent fan guide

This is an unofficial fan information site about the Firefly Wedding manga and anime. We help English-speaking readers find official announcements, understand the premise without later-story spoilers, identify the cast, and reach licensed watching and reading services.

## Who maintains it

The site is maintained by its independent site editor, reachable at [support@fireflywedding.org](mailto:support@fireflywedding.org). “Site editor” refers to this website’s maintainer; it is not a role with the publisher, studio, broadcaster or licensing platforms. Editorial questions, factual corrections and rights requests all go through our [contact page](/contact).

## How information is checked

We check claims individually and place a source beside the relevant fact, table row or paragraph. Our source directory is:

- [Official anime broadcast information](https://hotaru-anime.com/onair/): Japanese channels and the announced weekly time slots. A weekly slot alone does not prove a particular episode’s date.
- [Fuji TV’s programme page](https://www.fujitv.co.jp/b_hp/hotaru-anime/): dated broadcast listings. These can change, so the page’s check date matters.
- [Official episode summaries](https://hotaru-anime.com/episodes/): episode numbers, Japanese titles and brief story premises. Our English summaries are paraphrases; our title glosses are not official English titles.
- [Official character profiles](https://hotaru-anime.com/character/) and [staff and cast credits](https://hotaru-anime.com/#castStaff): roles and Japanese performances. Cast announcements do not establish first-appearance episode numbers.
- [david production’s announcements](https://davidproduction.jp/news/detail/?id=13): studio news and casting announcements, checked against the anime site where possible.
- [Official comics directory](https://hotaru-anime.com/comics/), [Shogakukan’s series page](https://urasunday.com/title/2169) and [VIZ Media’s English catalogue](https://www.viz.com/firefly-wedding): publisher information and links to legitimate editions.
- [Official anime X account](https://x.com/hotaru_anime_pr), [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/hotaru_anime_pr/) and [Fuji TV’s anime YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@noitaminaofficial): announcement leads. An inaccessible or unverified social post is not enough to fill a missing field.

Missing dates, episode totals, casting or adaptation mappings are marked **not announced yet** when we cannot verify them from the linked official sources. This describes the limits of our checked sources, rather than a guarantee that no announcement exists elsewhere. We do not turn fan speculation into a release calendar.

## Copyright and artwork

Firefly Wedding and all related characters are the property of their respective rights holders. This is an unofficial fan information site and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the rights holders.

The official anime site identifies Oreco Tachibana, Shogakukan and the anime production committee in its [copyright notice](https://hotaru-anime.com/). We do not reproduce anime frames, manga panels, character portraits or subtitled images. Artwork whose permission cannot be verified has been removed. Text layouts and information cards are created for this site. Links to official trailers, previews, stores and streaming services open those services; we do not embed or mirror their media.

## Corrections and accountability

Sources may be updated after our review. If a fact is wrong or a link no longer supports it, send the page URL, the relevant sentence and a primary-source link through [/contact](/contact). Rights holders may request correction or removal through the same channel. We review the request and correct or remove the affected material promptly.
