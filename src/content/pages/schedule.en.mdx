## What “Friday 24:00” means

The Japanese distribution notice uses broadcast-day notation: Friday 24:00 is Saturday 00:00 in Japan. It marks the beginning of sequential distribution, not a promise that every service releases the episode at that minute. It also does not establish the Crunchyroll release time outside Japan. [Official distribution notice](https://hotaru-anime.com/onair/).
