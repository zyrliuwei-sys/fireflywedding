## Role and background

**Kōtarō Ogawa** (小川康太郎): Satoko’s childhood friend and bodyguard. The official profile identifies Kōtarō through a longstanding connection with Satoko and his role in protecting her. It says that he supports her aspirations. [Official character profile](https://hotaru-anime.com/character/).

## Known story line — opening premise only

His premise-level position is on Satoko’s side as a familiar protector. We do not expand this into a rescue chronology or later relationship outcome without a corresponding official source. Sources: [official story introduction](https://hotaru-anime.com/story/) and [official character profile](https://hotaru-anime.com/character/).
