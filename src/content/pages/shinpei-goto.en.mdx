## Role and background

**Shinpei Gotō** (後藤進平): The assassin at the centre of the story opposite Satoko. Shinpei was born on Tennyo Island and earns his living through killing. The official character introduction connects his assignment against Satoko to Mitsueda and describes an intense, jealous attachment to people he likes. [Official character profile](https://hotaru-anime.com/character/).

## Known story line — opening premise only

Satoko’s proposal brings the assassin into an unexpected relationship with the person whose life is threatened. This profile stops at that setup and does not describe later rescues, injuries or the ending. Sources: [official story introduction](https://hotaru-anime.com/story/) and [official character profile](https://hotaru-anime.com/character/). We omit later developments even when a publisher’s sales description discusses them.
