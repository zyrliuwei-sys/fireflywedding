## Role and background

**Jūbei Kirigaya** (桐ヶ谷十兵衛): Satoko’s father and an important part of her family background. The official profile describes Jūbei as Satoko’s ally within the family. He wants her to marry into a distinguished household with enough support for her medical treatment. [Official character profile](https://hotaru-anime.com/character/).

## Known story line — opening premise only

His hopes help explain why marriage and family responsibility matter in Satoko’s introduction. This page does not describe later family actions, visits or revelations. Sources: [official story introduction](https://hotaru-anime.com/story/) and [official character profile](https://hotaru-anime.com/character/). We omit later developments even when a publisher’s sales description discusses them.
