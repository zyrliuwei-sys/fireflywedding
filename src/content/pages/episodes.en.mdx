## How to read the date column

\*For episodes 2–5, an individual broadcast date was not verified in the primary sources checked for this guide. The required placeholder is **not announced yet**; it means the date is unverified here, even though an episode synopsis exists. We do not calculate historical air dates by adding seven days to the premiere.

Episode 1's date comes from the [official on-air page](https://hotaru-anime.com/onair/). Episode 6's date comes from [Fuji TV’s dated listing](https://www.fujitv.co.jp/b_hp/hotaru-anime/). The listed date is for Japan, not the date that an overseas service necessarily makes the episode available.

## Titles, future episodes and season length

The Japanese titles are the identifiers printed by the official episode pages. The English wording is this site's short reading aid, not an announced English release title. An official English title should be checked on the licensed platform.

Further episode titles, individual dates and summaries: **not announced yet** in the episode sources checked here. [Aniplex’s official home-video listing](https://www.aniplex.co.jp/hotaru-anime/) plans episodes 1–6 in the first box and 7–12 in the second. That supports a planned twelve-episode release, but does not establish the broadcast dates, titles or plot of episodes 7–12.
