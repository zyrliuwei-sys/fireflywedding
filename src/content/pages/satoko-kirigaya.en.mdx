## Role and background

**Satoko Kirigaya** (桐ヶ谷紗都子): The central heroine and daughter of the Kirigaya earl’s household. Satoko wants to marry into a distinguished family for her father’s sake, while living with a serious illness. Her official profile also emphasises her determination to prepare herself for that future. [Official character profile](https://hotaru-anime.com/character/).

## Known story line — opening premise only

When her life is threatened after an abduction, she proposes marriage to Shinpei as an immediate survival tactic. The proposal is the opening premise, not confirmation of how their relationship ends. Sources: [official story introduction](https://hotaru-anime.com/story/) and [official character profile](https://hotaru-anime.com/character/). We omit later developments even when a publisher’s sales description discusses them.

## A spoiler-light way to follow this character

The useful distinction in Satoko’s introduction is between a family-directed future and a choice made under immediate pressure. Readers can follow that tension without knowing the identity behind the abduction or the outcome of the relationship. This is our reading of the stated premise, not an additional plot event.
