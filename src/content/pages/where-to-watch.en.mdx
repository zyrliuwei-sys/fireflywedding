## Open the official platforms

- [Official Japanese broadcast and streaming directory](https://hotaru-anime.com/onair/) — includes outgoing links for the domestic services, plus the full subscription and rental lists.
- [Crunchyroll title search](https://www.crunchyroll.com/search?q=Firefly%20Wedding) — this is a search link, not proof of a particular region's catalogue or a verified series landing page.
- [TVer](https://tver.jp/) and [ABEMA](https://abema.tv/) — check each service for the current free window and local access rules.
- [Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/), [Prime Video Japan](https://www.amazon.co.jp/Prime-Video/b?node=3535604051) and [U-NEXT](https://video.unext.jp/) — check the current catalogue before paying for access.

These links take you off this site. No player, trailer or episode is embedded, downloaded or mirrored here.

## Regional and subtitle checks

For countries outside the Japanese domestic listing, country-by-country rights are **not announced yet** in the primary sources verified for this table. We do not label a platform “global” or recommend a different region's catalogue as a workaround.

A Japanese service listing does not establish English subtitles. The source list does not specify subtitle tracks for each service, so those cells say **not announced yet**. Check the title's language selector and access terms on the platform in your own region. Audio language and subtitle language are separate checks.
