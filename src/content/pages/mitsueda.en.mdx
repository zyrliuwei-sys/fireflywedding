## Role and background

**Mitsueda** (三枝): A Tennyo Island yakuza involved in the abduction setup. The official introduction says that Mitsueda is hired by an unidentified party to abduct Satoko. It presents him as willing to pursue money by harsh means, while also having a more sympathetic side. [Official character profile](https://hotaru-anime.com/character/).

## Known story line — opening premise only

His link to the abduction places him in the opening chain of events. The identity and motives of the person behind the request are outside this spoiler-light profile. Sources: [official story introduction](https://hotaru-anime.com/story/) and [official character profile](https://hotaru-anime.com/character/). We omit later developments even when a publisher’s sales description discusses them.
