## The same story in two formats

Firefly Wedding's original manga is by Oreco Tachibana. The anime's [official story introduction](https://hotaru-anime.com/story/) presents Satoko's abduction and her marriage proposal to Shinpei as the opening situation. This guide compares entry points, rather than claiming every scene follows the same order.

## Our reading advice

**Start with manga Volume 1 if you want the original sequence.** This is editorial advice: beginning at the start avoids relying on an unverified “continue from chapter” number. It also lets you compare the presentation for yourself without treating a promotional synopsis as a complete adaptation record. Use [VIZ's series page](https://www.viz.com/firefly-wedding) for its English edition and official retailer links, or [Shogakukan's series page](https://urasunday.com/title/2169) for the Japanese publication.

**Start with anime episode 1 if you prefer a performed introduction.** Check [Where to watch](/where-to-watch) for officially sourced service information. Neither format requires reading later-volume summaries first. Publisher sales descriptions may reveal later developments, so open those pages with that in mind.

## Why there is no “skip to this chapter” claim

A synopsis can confirm an episode's premise without identifying every chapter used. A season can also rearrange or omit material. Until an official source establishes the mapping, this site does not present a chapter number as a safe continuation point. Our practical recommendation is to start at Volume 1, or keep your own place while comparing the two formats.
