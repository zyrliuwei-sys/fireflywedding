## How these roles fit together

“Original manga” identifies the source work. The director, series composition and screenplay, character design, and animation production are separate credits in the official listing. We retain that distinction so a reader searching for a particular credit does not have to infer it from a studio name.

The studio's [2 August announcement](https://davidproduction.jp/news/detail/?id=13) separately confirms the casting of Kōtarō and Mitsueda. For the complete production roster and any later additions, follow the original credits rather than treating this selected table as an exhaustive list.

## Unconfirmed fields and other languages

Mitsuharu Okumura's anime role and casting: **not announced yet** in the anime profiles checked for this guide. We have not inferred a performer from fan cast lists. This page covers Japanese performances; it does not assign these actors to an English dub.

First-appearance episode numbers belong on the [character profiles](/characters). Where a primary source does not establish a first appearance, the profile says **not announced yet**. To report a changed credit, send the role and an official announcement link through [Contact](/contact).
