## Contact the site editor

Email [support@fireflywedding.org](mailto:support@fireflywedding.org). This mailbox is the contact channel for this independent fan information site, including factual corrections, broken links, accessibility issues and copyright questions.

We aim to reply within **two business days**. This is a response target rather than a guaranteed deadline. A request that needs source checking may take longer to resolve; urgent rights requests are prioritised.

## Copyright, correction and removal requests

**Rights holders may email us to request correction or removal of content, and we will handle those requests promptly.**

Please include the exact page or resource URL, identify the content concerned, and explain your relationship to the rights holder. If possible, provide a link to the original work or an official statement. We use these details to locate the material and assess the request. Do not send identity documents, payment details or confidential contracts in your initial email.

You do not need to use a special legal form to bring a concern to our attention. Where permission cannot be confirmed, our editorial policy is to remove the artwork and use a text notice. For the site's independence and rights statement, see [About](/about).

## Reporting a factual error

Tell us which sentence or table entry needs attention, what should change, and which official announcement, broadcaster listing or publisher page supports the correction. Please distinguish a changed schedule from a transcription error; both matter, but their sources may be different.

A useful subject line is “Correction: /episodes” or “Rights request: [page URL]”. We do not publish the sender's email address as part of a correction. We may update the affected page's check date when a verified correction changes its information.

## What this mailbox can help with

We maintain this guide's text and links. Streaming access, account billing, subtitles inside a platform, manga orders and publisher services are handled by the relevant official provider. Use the links in [Where to watch](/where-to-watch) and [Manga and anime](/manga-vs-anime) to reach them.
