> **Manga spoilers ahead.** This article discusses the final Japanese volume and the official side story.

**There’s no confirmed child of Satoko Kirigaya and Shinpei Gotō in the official material we could verify.** The boy who travels with Shinpei in the official side story is his pupil, Yataro Kawai. [Shogakukan’s official side-story listing](https://shogakukan-comic.jp/book?isbn=9784098548491)

## Who is Yataro Kawai?

Shinpei sets out after promising Satoko that he’ll use his abilities to help others. He meets Yataro on the road, and the boy decides to travel with him. The side story follows them in the years leading up to the main story’s finale, when Yataro’s company helps Shinpei begin to move forward. [Shogakukan’s side-story synopsis](https://shogakukan-comic.jp/book?isbn=9784098548491)

## What about the Volume 13 epilogue?

Volume 13 is the final volume of the Japanese main series. Its publisher listing describes two separate extras. One tells how Asagiri and Mitsueda met. The other is a five-page bonus manga featuring Satoko and Shinpei after the main story. The public listing gives no details about the couple having a child in those pages. The full bonus is the place to check what that scene actually shows. [Shogakukan’s Volume 13 listing](https://shogakukan-comic.jp/book?isbn=9784098548484)

## Where to read the ending

Look for Volume 13 for the Japanese ending and its five-page bonus. The Yataro story is collected in a separate Japanese _gaiden_ volume.
