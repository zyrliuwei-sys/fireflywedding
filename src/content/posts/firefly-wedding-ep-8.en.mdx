## Episode 8 preview — “Bodyguard”

> **Mild anime spoilers.** This preview covers the setup from Fuji TV’s synopsis.

Satoko nurses Shinpei until he regains consciousness. As the two grow closer, Kōtarō Ogawa turns up at Isekichi as a customer and says he intends to bring Satoko back. [Fuji TV’s episode 8 preview](https://www.fujitv.co.jp/b_hp/hotaru-anime/)
